SimRun: an open-source route planner and activity simulator

SimRun is a free web app for planning a running or cycling route, simulating what that activity would look like, and exporting the result as a GPX, TCX or FIT file. It runs entirely in the browser and has no account, backend, analytics or cloud history. The source is MIT-licensed and published on GitHub.

What SimRun does

  • Plan a route. Click on the map to place waypoints and let road, path and trail routing connect them, or import an existing GPX, KML or GeoJSON line. Routes can be reversed, turned into an out-and-back, closed into a loop, trimmed, split, merged or generated as a target-distance round trip.
  • Simulate the activity. Choose running, walking, hiking, trail running, cycling or mountain biking, set a pace or speed, and add heart rate, power, cadence, weather, fatigue, GPS noise and dropouts. Output is seeded, so the same seed reproduces the same activity.
  • Read the result. Distance, duration, pace, elevation gain, splits, rest stops, grade-adjusted pace, training load estimates and profile charts are computed locally.
  • Export or hand off. Download GPX, TCX, FIT, a printable cue sheet, an SVG profile, JSON, a ZIP archive or a full library backup, or pass a file to another app through the share sheet.

What SimRun deliberately does not do

  • It does not record real GPS traces. Every activity is labeled simulated or estimated.
  • It does not sign in to Strava, Garmin Connect or COROS, and it does not upload on your behalf.
  • It does not store anything on a server, so there is nothing to delete from an account.
  • It does not model traffic, offer satellite imagery, or provide translations beyond English.

Who it is for

Runners and cyclists preparing a route before a run; developers and testers who need realistic GPX, TCX or FIT sample files; coaches and clubs producing example activities for training sessions; and anyone who wants a route file without creating an account on a commercial platform.

Frequently asked questions

How do I create a GPX file without an account?
Open SimRun, click the map to add at least two waypoints, then press Export. The file is generated in your browser and downloaded directly to your device.
Can SimRun files be imported into Strava, Garmin Connect or COROS?
Yes, as files. Those services import GPX, TCX and FIT through their own upload pages or phone apps. SimRun itself stays account-free and only gives you the file.
Is SimRun free and open source?
Yes. SimRun is MIT-licensed. There are no paid tiers, no accounts and no runtime dependencies, and the source is at github.com/exterminatorrat/simrun.
Where are my routes and activities stored?
In your browser: IndexedDB for activities and drafts, localStorage for preferences, and Cache Storage for the offline shell. Export a JSON backup to move them to another browser or device.
Are the exported activities real recordings?
No. They are simulated and labeled that way in the interface and in the exported files. Pacing, heart rate, power, cadence and elevation values are estimates.
Which external services does SimRun contact?
Routing and elevation use the public FOSSGIS Valhalla service; the default basemap uses OpenFreeMap and OpenStreetMap tiles. Nominatim, Photon, Overpass, OpenTopoMap and Waymarked Trails are off by default and contact their servers only after you enable and use them.
