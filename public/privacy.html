Privacy

SimRun is a local-first app. It has no SimRun accounts, analytics or cloud history, and activities and drafts are never uploaded automatically. Its only server-side code is the optional Strava upload described under Sharing, which stores nothing beyond the connection cookies in your browser.

What stays in your browser

  • Your activity library and drafts are stored in IndexedDB on this device.
  • Preferences and app settings are stored in localStorage.
  • The service worker uses Cache Storage for the app shell and map or offline resources you view or choose to save.
  • Imported files are parsed locally. Simulation and export files are created in your browser unless you explicitly share or save them elsewhere.

When information is sent to providers

  • When you request routing or elevation, the selected routing or elevation provider receives the route coordinates needed to calculate the result.
  • When the map loads or moves, the configured map-style and tile providers receive requests that reveal the map area and zoom you view. The default basemap uses OpenFreeMap and OpenStreetMap resources.
  • If you choose the OpenTopoMap base layer or the Waymarked Trails cycling or hiking overlays in Settings, those tile servers receive requests that reveal the map area and zoom you view.
  • If you opt in to a search feature, Nominatim, Photon, or Overpass may receive the search query or coordinates needed for that request. Photon suggestions send what you type as you type; Overpass points of interest send sampled coordinates along your route when you choose Find places.
  • If you turn on surface and road-type data, the routed path is sent to the routing provider for that extra lookup.
  • Locate me asks your browser for your position only when you press it. The position is used on your device; it leaves your device only if you then route from it or search near it.
  • Provider requests also reveal your IP address and ordinary connection metadata to that provider. Provider privacy practices apply to those requests.

Sharing

A share link can contain route geometry in the URL fragment. The fragment is not sent in the page request to SimRun, but anyone you give the complete link to can read that geometry. Sharing a link or exported file is a choice you make.

SimRun never uploads your activity history. Garmin Connect, COROS and other services receive a file only when you hand it over yourself.

If the deployment enables the optional Strava upload, you can connect your own Strava account from the Send dialog. The authorization token is kept in an HttpOnly cookie for this site only, and the single file you choose is passed through this site's serverless function to Strava's upload API. Nothing is uploaded unless you press Upload to Strava, and Disconnect Strava revokes the access grant at Strava and clears the stored token. Strava's own privacy policy applies to the activity once it reaches your account.
